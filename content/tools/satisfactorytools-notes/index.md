A collection of planners and calculators for [Satisfactory](https://www.satisfactorygame.com/),
a factory building game. I made it to automate planning production lines, which is
incredibly tedious by hand.

- Planning
  - **Production planner:** several products in one factory, solved as a linear programme
  - **Recipes & items:** what makes each item, what it goes into, per-minute rates
  - **Alternate recipe comparer:** every recipe for an item, planned back to raw resources
  - **Milestone shopping list:** HUB milestones and Space Elevator phases still to deliver
- Machines
  - **Overclock & Somersloop:** machine counts and clocks for a target rate
  - **Miners & nodes:** extraction rates by purity and clock
  - **Power plants:** generators, fuel and water for a target
  - **Belt splitting:** any ratio out of any number of belts, exactly
- Logistics
  - **Trains & drones:** throughput of a route, Freight Cars for a target
  - **AWESOME Sink:** points per minute and when the next coupon prints
- Save any tool as a PNG or SVG image
