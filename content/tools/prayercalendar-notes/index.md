Prayer Calendar exports prayer times to a calendar file (`.ics`). I made it to see my
prayer windows on my calendar, find when I can catch each obligatory prayer, and plan
ahead on busy days when a window is narrow.

- Each prayer as an event lasting its whole window
  - Optionally the makrooh times and the gap between Fajr and Dhuhr
- Location from a city list, your browser, or coordinates from a map
- Calculation
  - Methods such as ISNA, Muslim World League and Umm al-Qura, or your own Fajr and Isha angles
  - Asr by Mithl, Mithlain or both
  - Rules for high latitudes
- Any date range up to a year, with a quick pick for the next Ramadan
- Each event renamed or switched off, with its own reminder; Jumu'ah on Fridays
- Day-by-day preview before you download
- Printable PDF timetable, Letter or A4
